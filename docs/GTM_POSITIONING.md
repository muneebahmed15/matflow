## Sales Battlecard vs 97Display

**They win on:** HyperLocal SEO (70+ directories), managed PPC, AI voice receptionist, 15 years of agency support.

**We win on:** Unified ops + marketing, BJJ depth, lower total cost, data ownership, instant lead-to-member conversion.

**Objection handling:**
- *"97Display has better SEO"* → We offer per-gym JSON-LD, dynamic sitemaps, GBP integration, and location pages. For directory sync, we partner with Yext rather than reinventing HyperLocal.
- *"We already use MSI"* → MatsFlow replaces both MSI and 97Display. One bill, one login.
- *"97Display builds our website"* → Our CMS + blog lets you own content; Growth tier includes automation they charge extra for.
